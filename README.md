# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

A Business Intelligence project built using 
1.SQL,
2.Microsoft Excel, and 
3.Power BI 
to analyze customer churn, identify key churn factors, and provide actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by subscription and service-based businesses. This project analyzes customer data to identify churn patterns based on demographics, contracts, payment methods, internet services, tenure, and geography.

The dashboard helps decision-makers understand why customers leave and which customer segments are at higher risk of churn.

---

## Key Findings

### Overall Customer Metrics

- Total Customers: **6,418**
- Total Churned Customers: **1,732**
- Churn Rate: **27%**
- New Joiners: **411**

---

### Customer Segments with High Churn

- Customers with **Month-to-Month contracts** show the highest churn rate.
- **Fiber Optic** users have significantly higher churn compared to DSL and Cable users.
- Customers using **Mailed Check** payment methods exhibit higher churn.
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to leave.
- Certain states contribute more heavily to overall churn.

---

### Major Reasons for Churn

The most common churn categories include:

- Competitor Offers
- Attitude
- Customer Dissatisfaction
- Price
